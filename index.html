Information required

  • Personal and contact details
  • Loan and lender details
  • Nature of harassment
  • Details of previous complaints, if any
  • Financial loss or employment impact, if applicable
  • Supporting evidence (optional)
  • Legal declaration and consent

Supporting documents

You may upload up to 10 supporting documents. Each file must not exceed 5 MB, and the combined upload size must not exceed 20 MB.

Important note for respondents

If you have multiple loans with different lenders or agencies, please submit a separate report for each loan.

Do not share passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, unmasked Aadhaar cards or unnecessary sensitive personal information.
